Image Matching Task

Participant instructions — MRI scanning session

What you will do

On each trial you will see three images on the screen: one image at the top and two images at the bottom left and bottom right. Your task is to decide which bottom image matches the top image, and press the corresponding button as quickly and accurately as you can. Look freely at all three images to make your decision — you do not need to keep your eyes fixed in one place during the trial.

There are two types of image blocks that alternate throughout the session: face matching and shape matching. A short text label appears at the start of each block to tell you which type is coming.

What the screen looks like

Below are examples of what you will see during each type of block. The three images are always arranged the same way: one on top, two on the bottom. A fixation cross (+) is always visible in the centre of the screen.

Note: The examples below are illustrations. The actual face photographs come from standardised face databases and show real people with different emotional expressions.

How to respond

You will hold a button box in your hand. Use the following fingers:

INDEX
finger
Left image matches Press when the bottom-left image is the correct match.
MIDDLE
finger
Right image matches Press when the bottom-right image is the correct match.

Tips

  • Respond as quickly and accurately as you can.
  • The images stay on screen for 4 seconds — try to respond within that window.
  • Between trials, a fixation cross (+) will appear on a blank gray screen. Rest your eyes on it while you wait for the next trial.
  • There are no trick trials — one of the bottom images always matches the top.

Important MRI instructions

⚠ Please read carefully

  • Stay as still as possible throughout the entire scan, especially your head. Even small movements reduce image quality.
  • The scan will begin automatically. You will see a blank gray screen for 10 seconds before the first block — this is normal, the scanner is preparing.
  • If you feel uncomfortable at any point, squeeze the alarm ball in your non-dominant hand to alert the MRI operator.
  • If you miss a trial and forget to press, do not worry — just continue with the next trial.
  • Try to blink naturally but avoid excessive eye movements between the fixation cross and the images.

Questions?

Please ask the experimenter if anything is unclear before the scan begins. Once you are in the scanner, you can communicate via the intercom at any time.
